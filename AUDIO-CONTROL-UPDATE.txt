MY STUDY STATION — REELS AUDIO CONTROL UPDATE

This package is based on the previous working Volume Control Update ZIP.

Audio behaviour:
- Each Reel starts muted.
- The centre control shows Play while a Reel is muted, even while muted autoplay is running.
- Tap the centre Play control to enable sound for the current Reel.
- Tap the centre Pause control to pause and mute the current Reel.
- When a different Reel becomes active, other Reels are paused and muted.
- The next Reel starts muted and waits for the student to tap the centre Play control for sound.
- The automatic 3-second sound change is not used.

Existing Supabase configuration, private student-reels Storage bucket, signed media URLs,
student_reels table queries, moderation filtering, and access controls are retained.

Upload the files from this ZIP using the same site-root replacement procedure as the previous
Reels update. Do not change Supabase Storage privacy or database policies for this update.
